### Best Result:

FID = 135.22 (DR-4 verification, decaying regression, 20K steps)

### Key Finding:
Constant regression weight (lambda_reg = 1.0 throughout) outperforms decaying
(1.0 -> 0.3) by 25 FID points at 20K steps. The LPIPS perceptual loss serves
as an essential mode-preserving anchor that the LoRA-based DM loss alone cannot
provide, since the fake score NCE accuracy remains ~50%.
