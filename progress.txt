### Best Result:
FID = 82.64 (standard, cosine LR, lambda_dm=5e-5, 35K steps, v6)
FID = 101.96 (standard, cosine LR, lambda_dm=5e-5, 50K sched @ 40K, v8)
FID = 105.28 (DR-8, cosine LR, lambda_dm=0.001, 35K steps, v7)
FID = 110.38 (DR-8, constant LR, 20K steps, v5c)

### Key Findings:
1. Cosine LR schedule is critical for long-horizon DMD training.
   Constant LR overshoots the optimum at 25K+, causing degradation.
2. Weaker DM loss (5e-5 vs 0.001) produces smoother optimization
   with cosine LR, yielding 82.64 FID at 35K.
3. LPIPS regression remains essential as persistent anchor.
4. Schedule length matters: 35K cosine > 50K cosine.
   The rapid final LR decay in 35K schedule is key.
   50K schedule keeps LR too high too long, causing oscillation.
