### Key Findings (All Sessions):
1. **Warmup duration is critical** for LoRA-based DMD: 2000 > 1000 > 200.
   Extended warmup stabilizes LoRA adapter before full LR engages.
   warmup=200 degrades after 25K (like constant LR).
   warmup=2000 lags early but achieves 33% better final FID than warmup=1000.
2. Cosine LR schedule prevents late-training degradation (constant LR overshoots at 25K+).
3. Weaker DM loss (5e-5 vs 0.001) produces smoother optimization with cosine LR.
4. LPIPS regression remains essential as persistent anchor.
5. Schedule length matters: 35K cosine > 40K > 50K cosine.
6. DR verification helps intermediate models but hurts converged models.
