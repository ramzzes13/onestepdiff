### Key Findings (All Sessions):
1. **PHASE-ALIGNED WARMUP is the breakthrough**: warmup=5000 (= Phase 1 length) gives
   FID 28.06, a 3x improvement over warmup=1000 (82.64). The DM phase never sees
   an increasing LR, preventing noisy DM gradients from destabilizing the generator.
2. **Non-monotonic warmup effect**: 5000 > 2000 > 1000 > 3000 > 200.
   warmup=3000 is WORSE than warmup=2000, suggesting alignment with Phase 1 matters
   more than warmup duration per se.
3. Cosine LR schedule prevents late-training degradation (constant LR overshoots at 25K+).
4. Weaker DM loss (5e-5 vs 0.001) produces smoother optimization with cosine LR.
5. LPIPS regression remains essential as persistent anchor.
6. Schedule length matters: 35K cosine > 40K > 50K cosine.
7. DR verification provides marginal improvement for the best model (28.06 → 26.64 with DR-8).
